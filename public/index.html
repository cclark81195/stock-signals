How the verdict works

Each indicator looks at the latest daily data and votes Buy (+1), Sell (-1) or Neutral (0):

  • RSI (14): below 30 buy, above 70 sell
  • MACD (12, 26, 9): MACD line above its signal line buy, below sell
  • 50 vs 200-day moving average: 50-day above 200-day buy, below sell
  • Bollinger Bands (20, 2): price in the bottom fifth of the bands buy, top fifth sell
  • Stochastic (14, 3): below 20 buy, above 80 sell

Votes are added up: +3 or more = BUY, -3 or less = SELL, anything in between = HOLD. Trend indicators and overbought/oversold indicators often disagree, so HOLD is the most common result.

P/E is the share price divided by the last 12 months of earnings per share; it's blank for companies with negative earnings. The sector average is the plain average P/E of every profitable S&P 500 company in the same sector, so it's the same yardstick whichever index you're browsing. P/E is shown for context only and doesn't affect the verdict.
